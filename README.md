## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer data in the financial technology (FinTech) sector to understand customer behavior, transaction patterns, financial performance, credit risk, fraud indicators, and customer retention.

The dataset provides information about customer demographics, banking activities, loans, investments, digital wallet usage, savings, and engagement. It can be used to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and financial behavior.
- Understand transaction patterns and customer spending.
- Evaluate credit scores, loan status, and customer risk levels.
- Identify potential fraud indicators and overdue payments.
- Analyze customer engagement and digital wallet usage.
- Study customer churn prediction scores and retention opportunities.
- Examine customer revenue and cross-selling opportunities.
- Develop interactive dashboards to communicate business insights.

## 🧾 Dataset Description

The dataset contains 33 columns organized into the following categories.

### 1. Customer Demographics
- `Customer_ID` – Unique customer identifier.
- `Age` – Customer's age.
- `Gender` – Customer's gender.
- `Region` – Customer's geographical region.
- `Employment_Status` – Employment category.
- `Education_Level` – Educational qualification.
- `Marital_Status` – Marital status.
- `Dependents` – Number of dependents.

### 2. Transactions and Banking
- `Account_Type` – Type of customer account.
- `Transaction_Type` – Type of financial transaction.
- `Transaction_Amount` – Amount involved in a transaction.
- `Card_Type` – Type of payment card.
- `Savings_Balance` – Customer's savings balance.
- `Avg_Monthly_Spend` – Average monthly spending.
- `Monthly_Income` – Customer's monthly income.

### 3. Loans, Investments and Credit
- `Loan_Status` – Current loan application or loan status.
- ` Loan_Amount` – Loan amount; the original column name contains a leading space.
- `Credit_Score` – Customer's credit score.
- `Investment_Amount` – Amount invested by the customer.
- `Overdue_Payments` – Number of overdue payments.
- `Risk_Level` – Assigned customer risk category.

### 4. Digital Engagement and Customer Retention
- `Digital_Wallet_Usage` – Level of digital wallet usage.
- `Subscription_Plan` – Customer's subscription category.
- `App_Logins_per_Month` – Number of monthly app logins.
- `Active_Status` – Customer's activity status.
- `Customer_Tenure_Years` – Duration of the customer relationship.
- `Referral_Used` – Indicates whether a referral was used.
- `Churn_Prediction_Score` – Score indicating potential customer churn.

### 5. Fraud, Compliance and Revenue
- `Fraud_Flag` – Indicator associated with potential fraud.
- `KYC_Status` – Know Your Customer verification status.
- `Insurance_Status` – Indicates whether the customer has insurance.
- `Revenue_from_Customer` – Revenue generated from a customer.
- `Cross_Selling_Products` – Number of cross-selling products associated with a customer.

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

### Customer Analytics
Analyze customer segments by age, gender, region, income, employment, and account type.

### Transaction Analysis
Examine transaction amounts, transaction categories, spending behavior, and account activity.

### Credit and Risk Analysis
Evaluate credit scores, loan status, overdue payments, and risk categories to identify patterns associated with financial risk.

### Fraud Indicators
Study records flagged for potential fraud and explore their relationship with transaction behavior and customer characteristics.

### Customer Retention
Analyze churn prediction scores, customer tenure, active status, and app engagement to identify customers who may require retention initiatives.

### Revenue and Cross-Selling
Examine customer revenue and the number of associated products to identify opportunities for customer relationship growth.

## 📌 Important Considerations

- Validate data quality before performing analysis.
- Check whether customer identifiers are unique before treating each row as a distinct customer.
- Confirm the meaning and scale of `Churn_Prediction_Score` before interpreting it as a probability.
- Treat `Fraud_Flag` as an indicator for investigation, not proof of fraudulent activity.
- Review the original `Loan_Amount` column name because it contains a leading space.
- Do not assume that the dataset represents verified real-world financial activity unless its source confirms this.
